[ANIMATION_STRATEGY]
1. **Pedagogical Animation Plan:** 
    - **Parent VGroup transitions:**
        - A `VGroup` will be created for the summary elements: `formula_group = VGroup(law_of_sines, summary_text)`.
        - Transition: `FadeIn(formula_group, run_time=2)` will be used to introduce the Law of Sines and its summary together. This transition helps emphasize the connection between the formula and the explanation, guiding the viewer's attention to see that they are related concepts. 
        - **Rationale:** This coordinated introduction allows viewers to comprehend the mathematical foundation before moving to applications, creating a logical flow from theory to practice.

    - **Element animations within VGroups and for individual Mobjects:**
        - **Formula Animation**: 
            - Animation: `Write(law_of_sines, run_time=2)`.
            - **Purpose:** This animation introduces the formula first, allowing viewers to see it being constructed, which enhances understanding and retention.
        
        - **Summary Text Animation**: 
            - Animation: `FadeIn(summary_text, run_time=1)` after the formula.
            - **Purpose:** Following the formula, this animation reinforces the concept by providing a clear verbal description, ensuring the viewer connects the mathematical representation with its meaning.
        
        - **Application Images Animation**: 
            - Navigation Image: `FadeIn(nav_image, run_time=1)`.
            - Engineering Image: `FadeIn(eng_image, run_time=1)` after the navigation image.
            - **Purpose:** The introduction of application images after the summary text helps viewers visualize real-world uses of the Law of Sines, making the learning relevant and grounded.

    - **Coordinate element animations with VGroup transitions:**
        - The animations will be sequenced: The formula appears first, followed by the summary, and then the application images. Each will have its respective `Wait()` between to allow for processing.
        - **Rationale:** This sequence ensures that each component builds on the previous one, facilitating a step-by-step understanding of how the Law of Sines can be applied.

2. **Scene Flow - Pedagogical Pacing and Clarity:**
    - **Overall animation sequence, spatial progression for learning:**
        - The scene will start with the formula, leading into the summary, followed by images representing applications.
        - The spatial arrangement will be such that the formula and summary are on the left, while the application images are on the right, reinforcing the concept-to-application flow.
    
    - **Transition buffers for pedagogical pauses:**
        - `Wait(2)` after the formula animation allows viewers to absorb the formula before introducing the summary. 
        - `Wait(2)` after the summary allows the audience to reflect on the meaning before moving to applications.
        - `Wait(2)` after each application image introduces a moment for viewers to consider how the Law of Sines is relevant in real life.
        - **Purpose:** These pauses encourage viewers to internalize each segment, promoting better retention and understanding.

    - **Coordinate animation timing with narration for engagement and comprehension:**
        - The narration will cue the start of each animation, for instance:
            - “Let’s summarize the Law of Sines…” as the formula appears.
            - “The Law of Sines relates sides and angles in any triangle…” as the summary fades in.
            - “For example, in navigation…” as the navigation image fades in.
        - **Rationale:** This synchronization keeps viewers engaged as they watch and listen simultaneously, reinforcing their learning experience.
